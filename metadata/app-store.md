Looking for authentic connections that turn into real-world moments? Welcome to Nazar, the modern dating and spatial social discovery platform engineered for genuine connection, mutual respect, and vibrant community life.

Nazar combines intelligent proximity matching with rich personality profiles, interactive communities, and curated local events, giving you a refreshing way to meet people who share your lifestyle, values, and passions.

KEY FEATURES:

SPATIAL SOCIAL DISCOVERY
Explore curated profiles of singles nearby. Filter by age, lifestyle, relationship intent, and distance. With our mutual match guarantee, conversations only begin when both of you express genuine interest.

REAL-TIME CHAT & MESSAGING
Enjoy seamless, instant messaging powered by real-time technology. Know when your match is replying with live typing indicators and delivery receipts.

EXPRESSIVE PROFILES & PROMPTS
Showcase your true self with curated personality prompts and multi-photo portfolios. Highlighting your interests, humor, and lifestyle has never been easier.

COMMUNITIES & LOCAL EVENTS
Go beyond individual profiles. Join interest-based communities (from climbing and arts to culinary adventures) and RSVP to curated local singles gatherings and mixers.

NAZAR PLUS & PREMIUM SUBSCRIPTIONS
Unlock unlimited discovery swipes, discover who liked your profile before matching, and gain enhanced visibility. Subscriptions are billed to your Apple ID account and renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.

SAFETY & PRIVACY FIRST
Your security is our highest priority:

- Exact GPS coordinates are never revealed or shared with other members.
- Robust, one-tap blocking and reporting mechanisms with active 24/7 moderation.
- Full self-service data ownership: delete your profile and account in seconds directly from the app.
- Verified 18+ community with zero tolerance for harassment.
