# Nazar Release Notes · Version 1.0.0 (Release Candidate)

Welcome to **Nazar 1.0.0**, our first production Release Candidate for Android and iOS! Nazar is a next-generation dating and spatial social discovery platform engineered for authenticity, mutual respect, and meaningful real-world connection.

---

## What's New in Version 1.0.0

### 1. Spatial Social Discovery & Matching

- **Interactive Discovery Deck**: Explore profiles of singles near you tailored to your lifestyle preferences, interests, and relationship intent.
- **Mutual Match Guarantee**: Connect only when both individuals express mutual interest.
- **Match Celebration**: Instant notifications and celebration modals when mutual matches occur.

### 2. Real-Time Chat & Connection

- **Encrypted Real-Time Messaging**: Lightning-fast messaging powered by low-latency WebSockets.
- **Typing Indicators & Read Status**: Transparent communication state so you always know when your match is replying.
- **Automatic Reconnection**: Seamlessly maintains conversation state during network handoffs between Wi-Fi and mobile data.

### 3. Rich Profiles & Media

- **Curated Prompt System**: Showcase your personality with prompts and thoughtful answers.
- **Multi-Photo Portfolios**: Upload and arrange up to 6 high-definition photos directly from your camera or photo library.
- **Profile Completeness Guide**: Real-time tips to help optimize your profile visibility and match potential.

### 4. Social Communities & Local Events

- **Interest Communities**: Join hyper-local communities based on shared passions, sports, arts, and hobbies.
- **Real-World Events & RSVPs**: Discover curated local singles mixers and group activities with real-time seat reservation.
- **Community Feeds**: Share posts and interact within safe, moderated community spaces.

### 5. Nazar Plus & Premium Subscriptions

- **Unlimited Discovery**: Experience unrestricted profiles without daily swiping limits.
- **See Who Liked You**: Instantly view singles who have already liked your profile.
- **Flexible Billing**: Seamless monthly subscriptions managed directly through Google Play or Apple App Store with one-tap purchase restore.

### 6. Safety, Trust & Privacy

- **One-Tap Reporting & Blocking**: Robust safety controls allow you to immediately block and report any profile or conversation.
- **Zero Coordinate Exposure**: Your exact GPS coordinates are never displayed or shared with other members.
- **In-App Account Deletion**: Full self-service data ownership: permanently delete your profile and account directly from Settings at any time.

---

## Reliability & Performance Improvements

- Cold app startup optimized under 1.2 seconds on contemporary devices.
- Battery-efficient location polling: updates only when discovery is actively viewed.
- Offline event and analytics queuing prevents data loss during cellular dropouts.
- Full support for dark mode and dynamic system text scaling.

---

## Known Limitations in Release Candidate

- **Audio & Video Calls**: Real-time peer-to-peer audio/video calling is scheduled for a future update; v1.0.0 focuses on text chat and rich photo sharing.
- **Web App**: Initial release candidate targets iOS (iOS 16+) and Android (Android 9.0+) native mobile applications.
